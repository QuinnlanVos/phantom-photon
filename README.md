# Phantom Photon

Neon wall-sliding puzzle game made in GDevelop.

**Play:** https://quinnlanvos.github.io/phantom-photon/

You are a photon of light that can only move along walls. Swipe along a wall to slide; past its end you keep flying until you hit the next wall. Violet ramps bend your flight 90°, pink launch pads kick you off the wall, and black holes bend your path (get too close and you're swallowed).

## Controls
- **Phone:** swipe to move, tap to launch off a pink pad, round arrow (top right) restarts the level.
- **Desktop:** arrow keys / WASD to move, Space / Enter to launch, R to restart.

## Source
The GDevelop 5 project is in `project/` (open `project/game.json` in GDevelop). The repo root is the exported HTML5 build.
